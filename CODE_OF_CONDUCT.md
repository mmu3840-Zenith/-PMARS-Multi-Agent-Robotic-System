# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We aim to maintain a professional, respectful, and research-focused environment.

Contributors should:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations.
- Avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior.
- Clearly distinguish evidence from speculation.
- Respect reproducibility and research integrity.

## Research Integrity

Claims about system performance should be supported by reproducible experiments or clearly identified as hypotheses, simulations, or future work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these standards.
